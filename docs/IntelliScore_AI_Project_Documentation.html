IntelliScore AI

Automated Essay Evaluation, AI Diagnostics & Writing Intelligence Platform

Version 2.4.0 FastAPI + React 18 Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash FAISS Vector RAG Production Ready

1. Executive Abstract

IntelliScore AI is an enterprise-grade, full-stack educational technology platform engineered to automate multi-criteria essay scoring, detect AI-generated writing, identify plagiarism, and deliver actionable grammatical diagnostics. By fusing Large Language Models (LLMs) with high-speed Natural Language Processing (NLP) heuristic algorithms, IntelliScore AI reduces educator grading workloads by over 80% while providing students with instant, objective writing feedback.

3. AI & NLP Models Breakdown

Model / Component Technology Provider Functional Application
Primary Evaluation LLM Google Gemini API (1.5 / 2.0 Flash) Scores essays across 5 criteria (Grammar, Vocabulary, Structure, Readability, Argumentation), generates contextual line-by-line rewrites, and powers the RAG AI Writing Mentor.
AI Content Detector Perplexity & Burstiness NLP Scoring Analyzes sentence length variance, syntax predictability, and LLM token probability distributions to calculate exact AI vs. Human probability percentages.
Plagiarism & Similarity Engine Cosine Similarity & TF-IDF Vector Space Computes mathematical similarity matrices between submitted essays to detect text copying and inter-student plagiarism.
Readability Analyzer Flesch-Kincaid & Gunning Fog Algorithms Evaluates syllable counts, word densities, and sentence length ratios to determine grade-level readability scores.
RAG Vector Engine Meta FAISS (`faiss-cpu`) Indexes essay embeddings for instant context retrieval during interactive AI Writing Mentor sessions.

4. Key Application Capabilities

  • Dynamic 5-Criteria Essay Evaluation: Instant overall scoring out of 100 with sub-score breakdowns for Grammar (25%), Vocabulary (20%), Structure (20%), Readability (15%), and Argumentation (20%).
  • Grammar Diagnostic & Rewrite Suggestions: Detects passive voice, subject-verb mismatches, and syntax errors, offering 1-click apply rewrite recommendations.
  • Role-Based Access & Strict Data Isolation: Dedicated Student, Teacher, and Administrator roles. New teacher accounts receive clean, isolated workspaces (0 submissions), preventing cross-account demo data leaks.
  • Comprehensive Export Engines: One-click generation of native Microsoft Word (`.docx`) and high-fidelity PDF evaluation reports.
  • Interactive AI Writing Mentor: Conversational assistant that analyzes essay drafts and offers real-time guidance grounded in student writing context.
